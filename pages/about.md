## IUSS Digital Soil Thin Section Collections (DSTSC) working group
There has been growing interest in the preservation and digitization of thin-section
collections. Some progress has been made in developing websites/digital repositories; however,
large repositories remain largely inaccessible. Recently, the International Union of Soil Science
(IUSS) authorized the creation of the Digital Soil Thin Section Collections (DSTSC) working
group, an initiative focused on the digitization, standardization, and global accessibility of
archaeological and soil thin section collections.

## Iowa State Digital Inquiry Fellowship

The Digital Inquiry Fellowship provides graduate students with a paid, mentored opportunity to explore and integrate digital methods into their research or teaching. Over a seven-month period, fellows will develop original digital projects with guidance and support from a Digital Scholarship Librarian. Their work will culminate in a public presentation at the Digital Scholarship Symposium in March 2027.

Depending on their research interests and academic goals, fellows may engage with a variety of digital approaches, including text analysis and encoding, data visualization, geographic information systems (GIS) and spatial mapping, digital archives and exhibits, web-based scholarly projects, data modeling and metadata design, computational analysis of cultural or scientific data, and emerging technologies such as AI-assisted research tools and workflows.
